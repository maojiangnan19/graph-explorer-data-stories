        05 / The reveal

        THE LIMIT OF THE REPRESENTATION

        Pattern is not understanding.

        TF-IDF can distinguish documents. Cosine can compare their wording. A factorization can expose recurring terms. PPMI can show statistical association. None of these representations knows motives, irony, historical change, or what a relationship means to the people inside it.
