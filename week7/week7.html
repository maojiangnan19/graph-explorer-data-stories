THE CONCEPT

What does retrieval do?

It converts a query and each article into numbers, then ranks mathematical similarity. A higher score means a stronger relationship under this representation, not a perfect character match.

Why use keywords?

Keywords make the starting assumption visible. Changing them can change the ranking.

02 / How machines read text

Long pages become smaller, inspectable pieces.

Preprocessing makes text ready for a method. This lab lowercases alphabetic tokens and removes common stopwords for its TF-IDF vocabulary. Chunking then decides where a long article becomes a passage.

Raw article→Tokens→Chunks→Vectors→Ranked evidence

THE TRADE-OFF

Smaller chunks can focus on one idea but lose context. Larger chunks preserve more context but can mix relevant and irrelevant material. The passages below are actual output from the selected rule.

03 / From words to vectors

A representation turns text into geometry.

Vectors are ordered lists of numbers. Similarity compares their direction, not whether a machine has human understanding. Choose a view to separate the methods clearly.
